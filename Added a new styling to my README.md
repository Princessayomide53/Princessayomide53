## From Biochemistry to Frontend

I studied Biochemistry at **Oduduwa University**. The discipline stayed. The work changed.

I moved into frontend because I wanted to take complex products and make them feel clear, fast, and worth using. That’s still the standard I hold my work to.

## How I work

- Own features from architecture through production, not just the last 20% of the UI
- Care about the details that compound — typography, state, load time, accessibility
- Prefer clean, maintainable systems over one-off screens
- Work closely with design, backend, and product in Agile teams
- Comfortable switching between strategy and hands-on implementation

## What I’m focused on

- Frontend roles where craft, performance, and product sense actually matter
- Motion and spatial interfaces — GSAP, Framer Motion, Three.js
- Design systems and component libraries that make teams faster

Open to **full-time or freelance** remote work.
